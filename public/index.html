How to Play

Everyone rolls a hidden hand

Each player gets the same number of dice, rolled in secret. You only ever see your own — everyone else's are hidden until someone calls a bluff.

Make a bid

On your turn, claim how many dice of a certain face exist across everyone's hands combined — not just your own. "At least" is the key word: the real count just has to meet or beat it.

Raise the bid

Each turn you must either raise or call. A raise means a higher quantity (any face), or the same quantity with a higher face. You can't lower a bid or repeat it exactly.

Lose a die, keep playing

Whoever was wrong — the bidder if the count fell short, the caller if it held up — loses one die. Once you hit zero, you're out. Last player standing wins.
